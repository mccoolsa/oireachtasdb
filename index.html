Why the party labels matter

Parties as they were, not as they are

Members change party. When that happens, most archives quietly relabel their old speeches with their new party. This one doesn't — a contribution from 2020 carries the party its speaker held in 2020.

FF · 66,651 FG · 54,730 CC · 52,832 SF · 47,549 IND · 41,558 GP · 13,363 PBP · 12,770 LAB · 12,383 SD · 9,612 AON · 3,203 II · 1,440 I4C · 751 RDR · 205

Several parties share a colour family — Fianna Fáil, Sinn Féin, the Greens and Aontú are all green — so the abbreviation, not the colour, is what identifies a party. Colours are chosen for legibility and are not official party branding.
